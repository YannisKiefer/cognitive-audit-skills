# Contradiction Finder

## Belief vs Behavior

The highest-value section of any audit. Find collisions between what the
subject says they value and what their behavior shows.

Only report contradictions with strong evidence. Structure examples like
this - content comes from data, never assumption:

- "X is stated as the priority, but 63% of relevant sessions go toward Y."
- "Simplicity is requested repeatedly, but scope expands after start."
- "Model A feels fastest, but tasks completed via B show less rework."
- "Testing is called mandatory, but validation collapses under deadline."

Format every survivor as a table row:

| What I appear to believe | What behavior shows | Evidence | Consequence |

Prioritize these findings. They are nearly impossible to see from inside
a single session, which is exactly why they are worth surfacing.

## Strength -> Shadow Pairs

No generic strengths. Every pair needs evidence.

STRENGTH: the capability, with episodes.
WHY IT HELPS: observed outcome.
SHADOW SIDE: where the same behavior becomes excessive and costly.
EARLY WARNING: an observable signal that the shadow is starting.

Example shapes - format only, discover the real ones:

- fast experimentation -> rapid learning -> but constant switching blocks
  consolidation.
- deep research -> better decisions -> but delays cheap reversible
  experiments.

## When It Backfires

- Manufacturing contradictions from tone instead of measured behavior.
- Listing strengths without shadow sides. If no shadow exists yet, say
  so - that is also information.

## One-Line Memory

The gap between said and done is where the leverage hides.
