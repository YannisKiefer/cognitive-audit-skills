# The Method

One pipeline, seven stages. Every skill plugs into exactly one stage.

```
DISCOVER      source-discovery
   |
INTAKE        evidence-hygiene
   |
RECONSTRUCT   episode-method          (attribution + episodes)
   |
ANALYZE       cognitive-loop-map, framing-planning, calibration-audit,
              debugging-trees, failure-loops, bias-candidates,
              collaboration-audit, learning-tracker, attention-ledger,
              model-matrix, contradiction-finder
   |
SYNTHESIZE    blindspot-miner
   |
REPORT        report-writer
   |
FORWARD       prospective-kit         (falsifiable predictions)
```

## Non-negotiables

1. **Attribution before interpretation.** Classify every action as USER
   INITIATED, MODEL SUGGESTED, MODEL CAUSED, SYSTEM CONSTRAINT, or UNKNOWN.
   The model's behavior is never the human's behavior.
2. **Episodes are the unit of analysis.** Messages are noise; tasks traced
   across sessions are signal.
3. **Coverage is measured, never assumed.** Report platform, range,
   sessions parsed, missing data, confidence - or say "not available."
4. **Secrets never leave the corpus.** Redact values in all quoted
   evidence. Analyze locally; store derived observations only.
5. **The 3x3x3 bar.** A finding needs roughly 3 independent tasks across 3
   months and 3 different contexts before it outranks an anecdote.
6. **Confidence ladder.** HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / SPECULATIVE - and LOW
   stays labeled LOW.
7. **No clinical territory.** No diagnoses, no IQ, no personality typing.
   Work behavior only.

## Evidence standard

Every significant insight carries: observation, evidence, frequency, date
range, platforms, representative episodes, counterevidence, alternative
explanations, confidence, practical consequence.

## Success criterion

The reader repeatedly thinks "I didn't realize I did that," followed by
"now I know exactly what to do differently." Anything shallower fails -
see report-writer for the full fail list.

## What this method cannot do

Retrospective transcript analysis cannot establish causation, capture
offline behavior, or escape selection bias toward recorded work. It ends
with hypotheses to test prospectively (prospective-kit), not verdicts.
