# The Research Behind the Method

Everything in `skills/` is original phrasing distilled from the work below.
Established peer-reviewed findings are listed first per shelf; emerging
work is marked (emerging) and treated as provisional. No source text is
reproduced anywhere in this repo.

## The Honest Line

Retrospective transcript analysis cannot prove causation. Confounders,
selection bias, and missing data cap every claim. That is why the method
ends with falsifiable prospective predictions instead of verdicts - see
[prospective-kit](skills/prospective-kit/SKILL.md).
